**1. The Thirsty Crow** (16 chars)
A clever crow can't reach water in a pitcher, so he drops pebbles one by one to raise the water level until he can drink. Persistence and smart thinking solve problems.

**2. The Boy Who Cried Wolf** (21 chars)  
A shepherd boy repeatedly lies about wolves attacking his sheep for fun. When a real wolf appears, villagers don't believe him and his sheep are lost. Lying destroys trust.

**3. The Tortoise and the Bird** (24 chars)
A tortoise mocks a bird's simple nest, boasting about his shell home. The bird explains his nest welcomes family and friends, while the shell only fits one. Home is about love.

**4. The Fox and the Grapes** (20 chars)
A fox tries desperately to reach high grapes but fails. Frustrated, he dismisses them as sour. We often belittle what we cannot achieve to cope with disappointment.

**5. The Miser and His Gold** (21 chars)
A miser buries his gold and checks it daily. A thief steals it. His neighbor says unused gold is worthless like stones. Wealth has value only when it's used wisely.

**6. The Goose That Laid the Golden Egg** (35 chars)
A farmer owns a goose laying golden eggs daily. Impatient for more wealth, he kills the goose but finds no more eggs inside. Greed destroys steady good fortune.

**7. The Ant and the Grasshopper** (28 chars)
An ant works hard storing food for winter while a grasshopper plays and sings. When winter comes, the grasshopper starves. Hard work and preparation prevent hardship.

**8. The Cows and the Tiger** (21 chars)
Four cow friends graze together safely from predators. They fight and separate. A lurking tiger kills them one by one. Unity provides strength and protection from danger.

**9. The Fox and the Stork** (19 chars)
A fox tricks a stork by serving soup in shallow bowls. The stork retaliates with food in tall jars. The fox learns to treat others as he wishes to be treated.

**10. The Ugly Duckling** (17 chars)
An "ugly" duckling is rejected and mocked by everyone. Lonely and sad, he eventually discovers he's actually a beautiful swan. True beauty comes from within and takes time.

**11. The Wolf in Sheep's Clothing** (29 chars)
A wolf disguises himself in sheep's skin to trick and eat sheep. His disguise backfires when a shepherd mistakes him for a sheep and kills him for dinner.

**12. Two Silly Goats** (15 chars)
Two goats meet on a narrow bridge and fight instead of sharing. They fall into the river. Later, wise goats cooperate by letting one pass over the other safely.

**13. The Elephant and Friends** (25 chars)
A lonely elephant is rejected by forest animals for being too big. When a tiger threatens them, the elephant saves everyone. They realize friendship is about loyalty, not size.

**14. The Young Crab and His Mother** (29 chars)
A mother crab criticizes her son for walking sideways. When she tries to walk forward herself, she fails repeatedly. They learn to accept their natural ways with pride.

**15. The Lion and the Mouse** (21 chars)
A mighty lion spares a tiny mouse's life. Later, the grateful mouse saves the trapped lion by gnawing through a hunter's net. No act of kindness is ever too small.
